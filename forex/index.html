Understanding Currency Pairs

Currency pairs represent the exchange rate between two currencies. The value tells you how much of one currency is needed to purchase another.

Let’s break it down with an example:

If the GBP/USD pair is trading at 1.15, it means 1 British Pound is equal to 1.15 US Dollars.

  • If GBP/USD moves from 1.15 to 1.16, the Pound has gained strength over the Dollar.
  • If it drops to 1.13, the Pound has weakened compared to the Dollar.
