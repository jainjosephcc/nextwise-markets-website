FAQs

What devices support the Nextwise Markets MetaTrader5 platform?

MetaTrader5 is available for supported desktop, web, iOS and Android environments. Features can differ between platform versions.

Is MetaTrader5 suitable for a newer trader?

MT5 provides charting, order and account-management tools for different experience levels. Newer traders should first understand order types, leverage and the risk of CFD losses before trading.

Which credentials do I use to sign in to MetaTrader5?

Use the account number, trading password and server assigned to your trading account. Your client portal email and password are separate from these credentials. Check the server shown in Live Accounts before connecting.

What should I complete before placing a live trade?

Complete registration and identity verification, create your live trading account and wait for your deposit to be credited. Then connect to MetaTrader5 and confirm the account number, server and funded balance. Review the instrument specifications, order volume, margin and charges before submitting an order.

Can I use Expert Advisors on MetaTrader5?

Compatible Expert Advisors can be used where your account and platform environment permit automated trading. Review the Trading Activity Compliance Policy before using a strategy; automated trading must meet the same account conditions and compliance requirements as other trading.
