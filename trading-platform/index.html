FAQs

What devices support the Nextwise MetaTrader 5 platform?

MetaTrader 5 is available for supported desktop, web, iOS and Android environments. Features can differ between platform versions.

Is MetaTrader 5 suitable for a newer trader?

MT5 provides charting, order and account-management tools for different experience levels. Newer traders should first understand order types, leverage and the risk of CFD losses before trading.
