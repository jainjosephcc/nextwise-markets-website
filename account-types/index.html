Your account questions, answered

Is my client portal login the same as my MetaTrader5 login?

No. Your client portal login gives you access to your profile, verification, funding and account management. Each MetaTrader5 trading account has its own account number, password and server details. Your account number and generated passwords are sent to your registered email. Choose desktop, web or mobile access, sign in with those trading credentials and check that you are connected to your funded live account.

Which deposit method should I use?

Open Deposits in the client portal to see the methods available to your account. Select your live trading account first, then review the method's minimum, supported currency and current payment instructions. Account minimums and payment-method minimums can differ. Use a payment source in your own name and submit any requested proof. A request or receipt does not mean funds are available to trade: check the deposit status and credited balance. Read the Deposit & Withdrawal Policy and Risk Disclosure before funding.

Can I trade while my deposit is still pending?

A pending deposit is not a confirmed trading balance. Wait for the funds to be credited to your selected live account, then check the balance before placing an order.

Where do I check verification and deposit progress?

In Settings, open Identity Verification and submit your identity document, proof of address and selfie with your ID as requested. Wait for approval and follow any document-replacement instructions. Review deposits in the portal's transaction history and check the credited balance in Live Accounts.

How do Standard, Pro, Premium and Raw accounts differ?

The account comparison above sets out the minimum initial deposit, spread model and commission for each account. All four use MetaTrader5. Review the current conditions in the client portal before selecting and funding an account, as eligibility and instrument availability can vary.

What should I check before my first trade?

Review the instrument specifications, market hours, volume, margin and charges. Check your order and risk controls before submitting, then monitor positions and activity in MetaTrader5.
