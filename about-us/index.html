Our story

Nextwise Markets was born from a simple yet powerful observation - the world of trading was either too complex or too closed off. Most platforms catered to experts, came with steep learning curves, or hid essential tools behind paywalls.

We built Nextwise to reduce those barriers through connected account access, market information, MetaTrader 5 tools and client support for both newer and experienced traders.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To make online trading clearer and more accessible through dependable technology, transparent information and responsive client support.

Our vision

To create a trading experience where clients can understand their account, access the tools they need and make informed decisions across supported markets.

What drives us forward

At the heart of Nextwise lies a simple purpose, to build a trading experience that genuinely works for people. Everything we create, improve, or stand for is shaped by our values.

FAQs

What is Nextwise Markets?

Nextwise Markets provides access to supported CFD markets through MetaTrader 5, alongside account management and client support. Availability and trading conditions vary by account and region.

How do I open an account?

Open the secure client portal, complete the registration form and provide the documents requested for identity and eligibility checks. Once approved, review your account conditions and available funding methods before connecting to MT5.

Which financial instruments may be available?

Available CFDs can include forex pairs, indices, metals, energy products and selected cryptocurrencies. The exact product list depends on your account and region; review current symbols and specifications in MT5.

How can I deposit or withdraw funds?

Log in to the client portal to review the funding methods available to your account. Processing times and provider availability can vary; contact Client Services if you need help.
