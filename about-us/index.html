Our Story

Nextwise Markets was born from a simple yet powerful observation - the world of trading was either too complex or too closed off. Most platforms catered to experts, came with steep learning curves, or hid essential tools behind paywalls.

How do I start trading with Nextwise?

Create an Account : Visit the official Nextwise website. Register Here Click on "Register" or "Open Account" to begin. Fill in your personal and contact details as required. Accept the terms and conditions to proceed. Verification (KYC) : Upload valid identity documents for verification in compliance with global KYC regulations. Deposit Funds : Log in to your account and navigate to the funding section. Select a payment method and deposit the desired amount. Select a Trading Platform : Nextwise supports MetaTrader 5 (MT5). Download and install the platform to begin. Login to the Trading Platform : Launch MT5 and log in using your account credentials. If you need assistance at any step, feel free to contact our support team .
