Restricted Countries: common questions

Are Nextwise services available in every country?

No. This page identifies countries and territories where services are not provided, including EU member countries. Review the full list above before starting registration.

Do the account restrictions apply to where I live?

The policy states that accounts are not offered to residents of the countries listed in its restricted-country section. Provide accurate residency details during registration and ask Client Services if your situation needs clarification.

Does being outside the list guarantee account approval?

No. Account opening also involves identity verification, due diligence and an eligibility assessment, as explained in the Client Services Agreement. Review the account-opening requirements as well as the country restrictions before applying.

Where can I read the full Restricted Countries?

The full document is provided above. Use “On this page” to find a topic, or browse the legal documents menu for related policies.

Who can clarify a point in this document?

Contact Client Services and mention the document title and section you would like explained. Read the full document before making decisions based on a summary.
