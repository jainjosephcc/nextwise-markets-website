Metals like gold and silver have long been trusted stores of value and safe-haven assets. Adding metals to your portfolio can help balance risk and protect against market volatility.

Portfolio Protection

Inflation Hedge

What Are Metal CFDs?

Metal CFDs (Contracts for Difference) let you speculate on price movements of metals like gold or silver without owning the physical metal. You profit when prices move in your favor.

Here's a simplified example:

  • If gold is priced at $1,800 per ounce and rises to $1,850, you earn from the price increase.
  • If it falls to $1,750, you face a loss unless you take a short position.
