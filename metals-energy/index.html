Why trade metals and energy?

Metals and energy prices respond to different economic, geopolitical and supply-demand factors. CFDs can provide flexible market exposure, but they also involve significant risk.

Distinct market drivers

Metals and energy prices can respond differently to currencies, interest rates, inventories and geopolitical events.

Supply and inventory data

Energy and industrial-metal markets can react to production changes, storage levels and transport disruptions.

Global demand

Industrial activity, investment demand and economic expectations can all influence commodity prices.

Visible trading conditions

Review the spread, contract specification and applicable commission for the selected instrument and account.

What are metals and energy CFDs?

These contracts track price movements in an underlying metal or energy market without transferring ownership of the physical asset. Gains and losses depend on the direction of the position and market movement.

Illustrative price example

  • If a gold CFD moves from USD 1,800 to USD 1,850, a long position gains value while a short position loses value.
  • If it moves from USD 1,800 to USD 1,750, the reverse applies. Fees and execution prices also affect the result.

Metals and energy FAQs

What is a metals or energy CFD?

These CFDs track price movement in an underlying commodity market without transferring ownership of the physical asset. CFDs are leveraged products and can result in substantial losses.

Which metals and energy CFDs may be available?

Availability can include CFDs linked to precious metals and energy markets and may vary by account type and jurisdiction. Review the current symbol list in MT5.

Do I own the physical commodity?

No. A commodity CFD tracks price movement without transferring ownership or arranging delivery of the underlying metal or energy product.

When can these CFDs be traded?

Trading sessions depend on the individual symbol and can include daily breaks or holiday changes. Check the current contract specification and session in MT5.

What can move metals and energy prices?

Prices can react to currency moves, interest-rate expectations, inventories, supply disruptions, geopolitical events and changes in demand. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.
