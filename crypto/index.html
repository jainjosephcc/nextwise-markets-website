What Are Cryptocurrencies?

Cryptocurrencies are digital currencies that operate on decentralized blockchain networks. They allow peer-to-peer transactions without banks or central authorities.

Here's a simplified example:

Bitcoin (BTC) is a digital currency. If BTC is valued at $50,000, that means you need $50,000 to purchase 1 BTC.

  • If BTC rises from $50,000 to $52,000, it’s gaining value.
  • If it falls to $48,000, the price is declining due to market pressure or selling activity.
