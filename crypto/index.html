Understanding cryptocurrency prices

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets whose prices can change rapidly. A cryptocurrency CFD follows the underlying market price without transferring ownership of the digital asset.

Illustrative price example

If BTC/USD is quoted at USD 50,000, that is an indicative underlying-market price. Trading a BTC/USD CFD does not mean purchasing one bitcoin.

  • If BTC rises from $50,000 to $52,000, it’s gaining value.
  • If it falls to $48,000, the price is declining due to market pressure or selling activity.

Crypto CFD FAQs

What is a cryptocurrency CFD?

A cryptocurrency CFD tracks price movement without transferring ownership of the underlying digital asset. Availability varies by account and region, and leveraged CFDs involve substantial risk.

Which cryptocurrency CFDs may be available?

The available symbols can change and may vary by account type and jurisdiction. Review the current crypto CFD list and each symbol specification in MT5.

Do I own cryptocurrency when trading a CFD?

No. A cryptocurrency CFD provides exposure to price movement without transferring the underlying digital asset or requiring a crypto wallet.

Can crypto CFD trading hours change?

Yes. Symbol sessions, maintenance windows and holiday schedules can differ. Check the current trading session shown in MT5 before placing an order.

Why is crypto CFD risk particularly high?

Cryptocurrency prices can move sharply, liquidity can change quickly and leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Review the risk disclosure and use risk controls appropriate to your circumstances.
