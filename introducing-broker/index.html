Frequently asked questions

What is an Introducing Broker (IB)?

An Introducing Broker is an approved partner who refers eligible clients to Nextwise Markets under a written partnership agreement.

How are IB commissions calculated?

Commission depends on the terms of your agreement and the qualifying activity of referred clients. The partnership team will explain rates, exclusions and the payout process before approval.

How do I apply to become an Introducing Broker?

Submit your partnership details for review. The partnership team will confirm eligibility, regional availability, commission terms and permitted marketing activity. Review and agree the applicable terms before starting your referral activity.

How can I monitor my referrals?

Approved partners use the assigned referral journey and reporting tools to monitor referrals, qualifying client activity and commission information. The available reporting access is confirmed during partner onboarding.

Where can I check commission payout information?

Use the available partner workflow to review eligible balances and payout status. Commission eligibility and payouts are governed by your partnership agreement; the partnership team can clarify the terms and payout process.
