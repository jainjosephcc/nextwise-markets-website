How the Introducing Broker programme works

  1. Apply. Submit your partnership details for review.
  2. Agree terms. Review eligibility, commission terms and permitted marketing activity.
  3. Refer and monitor. Use your assigned referral journey and reporting tools to follow qualifying activity.

Partner approval, commission eligibility and payouts are governed by the applicable agreement.

About the Nextwise partner programme

The programme gives approved Introducing Brokers a documented referral path, activity reporting and access to partnership support. Eligibility, permitted marketing activity, commission terms and regional availability are confirmed in the applicable agreement.

Frequently asked questions

What is an Introducing Broker (IB)?

An Introducing Broker is an approved partner who refers eligible clients to Nextwise Markets under a written partnership agreement.

How are IB commissions calculated?

Commission depends on the terms of your agreement and the qualifying activity of referred clients. The partnership team will explain rates, exclusions and the payout process before approval.
